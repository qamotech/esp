🤔 Should You Filter?

Why filter at all? Tap vs. eSpring

Municipal water is treated before it reaches your street — then it travels through pipes, and picks up taste, odor and whatever the journey adds. Filtering at the tap is filtering at the last step before your glass. Drag the slider to compare.

🚰 Unfiltered tap water

  • Taste & odor: chlorine used in treatment is still in the glass
  • No final barrier: nothing between aging pipes and your family
  • Bottled-water fallback: ongoing cost, plastic waste, hauling cases home
  • Unknowns stay unknown: no monitor tells you what's passing through
  • Minerals: present — but so is everything else

💧 eSpring™ filtered water

  • Better taste, odor & clarity: carbon block reduces chlorine taste and odor
  • Certified reduction: 170+ contaminants, incl. lead, mercury, PFOA/PFOS, microplastics*
  • UV-C LED treatment: destroys up to 99.9999% of bacteria, 99.99% of viruses, 99.9% of cysts*
  • Keeps the good minerals: calcium, magnesium, potassium, zinc pass through
  • One filter ≈ 10,000 bottles: 1,320 gallons before a once-a-year change

🚰 Before — straight tap

Chlorine taste, pipe journey, no last-step treatment. Fine for flushing — not what most families choose to drink all day.

✨ After — eSpring

Carbon-filtered + UV-C treated, minerals kept, monitored by a smart filter-life indicator and the Amway Healthy Home™ app.
